What's planned

Made for communities that run their own

Voice and chat for your group, on a server you control. No landlord, no small print.

Voice and video

Voice that sounds like you

Choose the audio quality for each voice channel, bitrate included, so music nights and raid calls both sound right. Video and screen sharing are on the way, on standard WebRTC.

Free, with nothing to unlock

No subscriptions, no boosts and no paywalled features. Snatter is open source under the AGPL, so the code stays open too.

Host yourself

Your server, your rules

Run it on a home server or a small VPS. One Docker Compose file brings up the server, the web app and the database. 

$ cp .env.example .env
$ docker compose up
# open http://localhost:8080

Privacy focused

Private by design

No ads, no tracking and no data sold on. Your messages, files and calls go through your own server and nowhere else, and even the bot check at sign-up runs there.

Checking you are not a bot… Verified on your server
  • Messages and files stay on your server
  • Voice runs through your own server
  • Bot protection without a third party
  • Open or invite-only sign-up, your choice

Built in the open

Snatter is in early development. Follow along, open an issue or send a patch. The first release is on its way.
